## 2. What a chargeback means

A chargeback is a payment reversal or dispute process raised through a bank, card issuer or payment network, distinct from a refund requested directly from us. Rights, deadlines and procedures may depend on the payment method, bank, network, provider and applicable law. Please contact us first for order, product, access or refund concerns so we can try to resolve them. This request does not limit lawful rights or require withdrawal of a dispute.

## 3. Our response process

We identify the transaction, compare payment and delivery records, review customer communications and respond through the relevant payment-provider process. Razorpay facilitates the payment relationship; it does not guarantee a transaction, refund, dispute decision or chargeback reversal. We cannot promise the outcome or decide every bank/card-network dispute ourselves. An open dispute does not automatically remove access. If the provider records a lost dispute against the payment funding the order, further download authorisations are restricted pending review; contact support about an error or a later reversal. This restriction does not remove mandatory rights.

## 4. Evidence we may provide

Necessary evidence may include order ID, payment ID/reference, date/time, amount and currency, product and listing snapshot, customer account/order information, verified payment status, access-grant timestamp, download-authorisation timestamp, relevant authorisation/security logs, relevant support messages, refund records and confirmation-email status where actually recorded. We minimise disclosure to information legitimately collected and needed for that dispute. We never submit passwords, full card numbers, CVV, OTPs, API secrets or payment-authentication secrets as evidence.

## 5. Non-delivery claims

We may distinguish captured payment, server verification, order creation, access being granted, a download being authorised and an email being sent. The store records authorisations, not a reliable record that a complete file was saved, opened or used on the customer’s device. We do not represent an issued link as conclusive proof of a completed download. If notification or actual access activity is not recorded, we do not invent it. A verified payment with no usable access is investigated for restoration or an appropriate refund.

## 6. Unauthorised transaction claims

We investigate available account, payment and security records without automatically accusing the customer of fraud. We cooperate with the provider, supply relevant evidence when requested, and may restrict access where credible evidence justifies it. Report suspected account compromise promptly. An allegation alone is not a finding of misconduct.

## 7. Product not as described

We compare the delivered product and version with the product listing captured at purchase, including included files, dependencies, limitations and license. We may request error details and offer genuine technical support, corrections or replacement files. Material differences that cannot appropriately be resolved are considered under the refund policy and applicable law.

## 8. Refund interaction

An existing refund request does not extinguish dispute rights. If we already issued a refund, we may provide the refund reference and amount to avoid duplicate recovery. If a chargeback is underway, we may coordinate through the provider before issuing another refund. We do not threaten customers or make a refund conditional on surrendering non-waivable rights. Contact support with the dispute reference where available so the cases can be matched.

Contact the support or grievance contact above with your order ID, payment reference, purchase date and a description of the problem.
