# Fraud Prevention & Transaction Security Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

## 2. Potentially suspicious activity

We may review unauthorised payment attempts, stolen payment instruments, payment-credential abuse, use of another person’s account or payment instrument without permission, multiple failed attempts, abusive multiple accounts and unusual transaction patterns. Technical failures, travel, shared networks and honest mistakes can also produce unusual patterns. No flagged transaction is automatically treated as fraudulent.

## 3. Payment and refund manipulation

Prohibited conduct includes fake or edited payment confirmations, manipulating payment status or a client-side success screen, bypassing the server’s payment check, and exploiting website/API vulnerabilities to obtain unpaid products. Deliberately false non-delivery statements, repeated refund abuse or knowingly abusive chargebacks may be investigated. A good-faith complaint, unsuccessful payment or exercise of lawful dispute rights is not abuse by itself.

## 4. Access and distribution abuse

We may examine account sharing, paid-link sharing, unauthorised redistribution, bulk or automated downloads, attempts to bypass authentication or authorisation, and unauthorised access to another customer’s account. For example, posting an expiring paid link publicly or repeatedly calling the download API to harvest files may justify restriction while the facts are checked.

## 5. Proportionate actions

Where reasonably necessary and subject to applicable law, we may temporarily delay delivery, seek relevant clarification, decline or cancel a suspicious order, limit download requests, restrict an account, preserve relevant records or cooperate with the payment provider. Restrictions should match the concern and available evidence. If a legitimate paid order cannot be supplied, the refund policy and applicable law remain relevant; restrictions do not authorise keeping money without a lawful basis.

## 6. Review and cooperation

Contact support if you believe a restriction is mistaken. Provide the order reference and relevant explanation. We review available records, correct errors and communicate an appropriate resolution, except where disclosure would undermine security or violate law. We may cooperate with banks, processors or competent authorities where authorised or legally required. Never send OTPs, passwords, CVV or authentication secrets to prove a purchase.

## 7. Security reporting

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to our support email with a concise description and non-sensitive reproduction steps. Do not access other customers’ data, disrupt the service or extract files beyond what is necessary to describe the issue. This policy does not promise a bounty or grant permission for intrusive testing.

## 8. Contact and resolution

Contact the support or grievance contact above with your order ID, payment reference, purchase date and a description of the problem. Do not send passwords, OTPs, CVV, full card numbers or payment authentication secrets. We review the available records and explain the appropriate next step. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.
