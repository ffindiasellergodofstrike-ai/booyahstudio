# Download, Access & Delivery Record Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

## 2. Records actually maintained

The store keeps order IDs, account association, product information, payment references/status, verification timestamps, access-grant timestamps and download-authorisation records. Authorisation records include product/order/account identifiers, time, expiry, and relevant IP/user-agent information. Support requests and confirmation-email status may also be retained when those operations occur.

## 3. What records mean

An access-grant timestamp means the account was entitled to request a file. An authorisation timestamp means a protected link was issued. Neither alone proves that the customer saved, opened or used a complete file. This store does not independently record a reliable completed-download or file-open event. We will not claim that unrecorded activity occurred or treat a sent email as proof it was read.

## 4. Uses and lawful sharing

Records support fulfilment, troubleshooting, fraud prevention, refund review and dispute responses. Relevant necessary records may be shared with payment processors, hosting/storage/email providers, advisers or competent authorities as described in the Privacy Policy. Payment authentication secrets are excluded from dispute evidence.

## 5. Retention and customer requests

We retain necessary transaction records for applicable accounting, tax, security, dispute and legal requirements, and review other records for deletion when no longer needed. A deletion request does not necessarily remove records that must lawfully be retained. Ask support to review an inaccurate record or an access problem; we assess requests and correct verified errors where appropriate.

Contact the support or grievance contact above with your order ID, payment reference, purchase date and a description of the problem.
