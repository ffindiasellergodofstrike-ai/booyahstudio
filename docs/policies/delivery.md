# Digital Product Delivery Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

## 2. What is delivered

We supply the downloadable digital files listed on the purchased product page. Products are delivered electronically; there is no physical consignment. Templates, scripts and source packages require the customer to use the supplied files in an appropriate environment. Buying a template does not provide a hosted service, third-party subscription or installation service unless the product expressly includes it.

## 3. Actual delivery channel

After successful server-side payment verification, the order becomes available in your signed-in customer account. A download action requests a protected link to the product ZIP in private Supabase Storage. Links expire after approximately 60 seconds; request a fresh link from your account if necessary. These links can be used during their validity and must be kept private. Confirmation emails sent through Resend link to the account; email is a notification, not the sole delivery method.

## 4. Verification and delays

Access is granted only after the server verifies a captured payment matching the stored order, amount and currency. Pending, failed, cancelled, test or unverified payments do not provide purchased product files. Browser success messages cannot override this check. Duplicate notifications are processed without creating duplicate purchase access. Verification, provider outages or credible security concerns may temporarily delay access; no universal instant-delivery guarantee is made.

## 5. Customer responsibilities

Use an accurate email and maintain access to your account. Keep download links private and check the product’s requirements before purchase. Save a secure backup of downloaded files within the permitted license. Expiring links are security controls and do not by themselves cancel valid purchase rights; contact support if account access or a link cannot be restored.

## 6. Delivery problems and examples

If payment succeeds but the account shows no access, send support the order ID and payment reference. For example, closing the browser after paying may leave the on-screen state pending while a verified webhook later updates the account. A missing notification email does not mean non-delivery if account access works. Conversely, a successful payment without usable access should be investigated and, if unresolved, reviewed under the refund exceptions.

## 7. Records and limits

We record access being granted and the issuance of download authorisations, with timestamps. Authorising a link alone does not prove that every byte was received or that a customer opened the file. Requests may be temporarily limited to prevent automated abuse; the current limit is 20 authorisation requests per product/account per hour. Contact support for a genuine need to retry. Suspension or refunds may prevent new authorisations; a previously issued link may remain usable until expiry.

## 8. Contact and resolution

Contact the support or grievance contact above with your order ID, payment reference, purchase date and a description of the problem. Do not send passwords, OTPs, CVV, full card numbers or payment authentication secrets. We review the available records and explain the appropriate next step. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.
